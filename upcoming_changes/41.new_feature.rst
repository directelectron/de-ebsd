:class:`~de_ebsd.index.live.LiveIndexer` took ``search="inplane"``, a dictionary search with the in-plane rotation factored out, which on simulated Ni, Ti and Fe scans at 12 counts a pixel left 0-0.21 % of patterns more than 5 degrees off where the flat dictionary at the same step left 0.06-0.73 %, indexed with ``fit=True`` 1.8-2.5 times faster at 2 degrees and 2.7-5.3 times faster at 1.5 and 1 degree on a Titan X, held 1/40 to 1/80 of the flat dictionary's memory, and built in under a second.

:class:`~de_ebsd.index.LiveIndexer` indexes EBSD patterns as a scan streams them, at over 8000 patterns a second on a GPU: a dictionary search against dynamical master patterns, then a refine of every orientation, with the static and dynamic backgrounds taken from the frames themselves. :meth:`~de_ebsd.index.LiveIndexer.refine_pattern_center` fits the pattern center shared by a batch to about 0.001, and :meth:`~de_ebsd.index.LiveIndexer.set_pattern_center` moves to a new center without rebuilding the dictionary.

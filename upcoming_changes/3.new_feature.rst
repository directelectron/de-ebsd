:meth:`~de_ebsd.Crystal.explore` opens a STEM orientation explorer linking a band-line sphere, the crystal structure, and a projection of geometric Kikuchi lines and kinematic spots, with no master pattern needed. :func:`~de_ebsd.index.hrebsd` measures small orientation and strain shifts by cross-correlating patterns against a reference, and :func:`~de_ebsd.index.remap_reference` rotates a reference onto a neighboring pattern first.
